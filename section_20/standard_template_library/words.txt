Dorothy lived in the midst of the great Kansas prairies, with Uncle Henry and Aunt Em.
The road to the Emerald City was paved with yellow brick; Dorothy walked along the road.
In the Emerald City of Oz, everyone wore green glasses to protect their eyes!
Dorothy asked the Scarecrow: "How far is the Emerald City from here?"
The Scarecrow smiled and pointed down the yellow brick road toward Oz.
Through the dark forest and across the river, Dorothy and her friends walked on.
At last, the gates of the Emerald City shone bright before Dorothy and Toto!
